About Me

An Luong is a Vietnamese artist and digital media art student based in San Jose, California. Originally from Vietnam, she brings her cultural background and personal experiences into her creative practice. Her work explores identity, memory, tradition, and the connection between Vietnamese culture and contemporary visual art.

An works across digital media, installation, sculpture, photography, and event design. She is especially interested in creating meaningful visual experiences that combine storytelling, cultural symbolism, and community connection. Alongside her studio practice, she works as an event designer specializing in floral arrangements, Vietnamese wedding tea ceremonies, and gia tiên decorations.
