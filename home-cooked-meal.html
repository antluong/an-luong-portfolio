Home-cooked Meal-Cơm Nhà

A Southern Vietnamese home-cooked meal often prepared for Tet: braised pork with duck eggs, bitter melon soup, pickled shallots, greens, and fish sauce. The round eggs symbolize fullness and prosperity. The square pork and round eggs together represent harmony between heaven and earth, expressing a wish for peace and abundance. Bitter melon, khổ qua, carries the hope that all hardships will pass, making way for good fortune. The richness of the pork and the slight bitterness of the soup reflect the balance of life, both sweet and bitter, warm and real. This artwork is a reflection of home, tradition, and love.
